# QFE V2.1 — Regularized Linear Pool Stage 1 V1

Status: **PREREGISTERED / DEVELOPMENT RESEARCH**

The ensemble is a convex linear pool, not a new predictor family. It is regularized toward the frozen V2 weights and selected by chronological expanding-fold validation on D2-D4. Competition-local strength is eligible only as a diversity component, not as a Layer-2 replacement. CMP is excluded after its negative Stage-1 result. CALIBRATION, PROTECTED, market prices and LLM inputs are forbidden.
